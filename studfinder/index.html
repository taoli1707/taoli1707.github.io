Why this is a simulation on iPhone: Safari doesn’t expose the phone’s raw magnetometer to web apps — only fused compass headings — so real sensing needs a native app (about 50 lines of Core Motion). This simulator runs the exact app flow: calibrate on blank wall, sweep slowly, watch the µT delta spike over hidden screws, mark and confirm vertically. On Android Chrome this page switches to live magnetometer mode automatically.
Real-world caveats the native app would show: magnetic finders detect the screws in a stud, not the wood; steel pipes and conduit read the same as studs; copper and PEX are invisible; MagSafe cases blind the sensor. Never drill on a single reading — confirm hits line up vertically, and keep clear of likely plumbing and wiring runs.
