From intermediate to dangerous

Warzone Field Manual

You already know how to loot, plate, and win a fair 1v1. The next tier is not about aim. It is about taking fewer fair fights, dying to fewer things you could have seen coming, and building repeatable habits. This manual is ordered by leverage: the chapters near the top fix the most deaths per hour of practice.

00 / Read first

Where the game is in September 2026

Half of "outdated advice" is advice written for a different ruleset. This is the ruleset the manual assumes.

The engine

  • Warzone runs on the Black Ops 7 integration (since Dec 4, 2025). BO7 weapons, attachments, perks and movement rules apply.
  • Season 5 launched Jul 23, 2026; Season 5 Reloaded on Aug 20. Season 6 (The Haunting) lands Sep 17 and will re-tune weapons. Modern Warfare 4 ships Oct 23, and history says a new Warzone integration follows within weeks.
  • Read the meta chapter as a snapshot with a short half-life. Read every other chapter as durable.

Modes that matter

  • Battle Royale: map rotation between Verdansk and Avalon; playlists cycle every few minutes across squad sizes.
  • Resurgence: Rebirth Island, Fortune's Keep, Haven's Hollow. Respawns while a teammate is alive. Best mode for reps.
  • Black Ops Royale: Avalon only. Blackout rules: no loadouts, no Gulag, no Buy Stations, consumable perks, respawn tokens and Redeploy Towers, grapple and wingsuit.
  • Ranked Play: Resurgence: trios, BO7 weapons only, Skill Rating ladder up to Top 250.

Movement rules (BO7 era)

  • Omnimovement: sprint, slide and dive in any direction.
  • No default Tactical Sprint. Base speed is higher; the Tac Sprinter perk brings it back.
  • Wall jump: chain up to three, each shorter than the last.
  • You cannot aim down sights while sliding, diving or wall-jumping unless a perk enables it. Slide into cover, never into the open.

Verdansk in Season 5

  • New POI Drone Labs east of TV Station, south of Quarry. It launches Supply Drones you can shoot down for loot; expect squads racing you.
  • Stadium is permanently unlocked and flagged as a High Value Loot Zone.
  • Gulag is a Rush-based layout that changes on weekends, with a "jailbreaker" event that frees both players if the fight drags.
The thesis

Intermediate players lose most of their games to decisions made 20 seconds before the gunfight: where they stood, what they knew, whether they had a way out. Aim decides who wins a fair fight. Good players rarely take one.

02 / Gunfights

Win fights before the first bullet

Most gunfights are decided by who saw whom first, who had cover, and who broke line of sight to plate. Aim comes fourth.

Pre-aim and centering

  • Crosshair at head height, on the next corner. Intermediate players look at the ground or the wall while moving. Keep the reticle where a head would appear, so the first shot needs a flick of centimetres, not a swing.
  • Pre-aim common angles when entering rooms, cresting hills or opening doors. If you know where someone would hold, aim there before you see them.
  • Peek with intent. Before you peek, decide: I am shooting, or I am gathering info and resetting. Peeking without a plan is how you get hit by the first bullet and then hesitate.
  • Range discipline. SMG under roughly 15 to 20 metres, AR from 20 to 60, sniper beyond. Swap weapons for the range instead of forcing the one in your hands. If you have the wrong weapon out, break line of sight first, then swap.

Cover discipline

  • Never take a fight without a fallback. The question before every shot: if I get cracked, which cover do I step into? If there is no answer, do not shoot yet.
  • Break line of sight to plate. Always. One plate takes about a second. Plating in a doorway or on an open roof is a free kill for whoever is watching. Step fully behind cover, plate, then re-peek from a different edge.
  • Head glitches. Waist-high cover exposes only your head, which shrinks the target massively. Fight from them; do not fight into them. Someone on a head glitch is flanked or grenaded, not out-aimed.
  • Reload behind cover only. If your mag runs dry mid-fight, swap weapons instead of reloading. Two seconds of reload in the open is the most common intermediate death in Resurgence.
  • Never re-peek the same edge after you've been shot from it. The enemy is pre-aimed there. Change height (crouch), change edge, or leave.

Controller aim assist works when you move

Rotational aim assist engages only while you give left-stick movement. Standing still while aiming turns it off. Strafe left-right while aiming, even a few centimetres, on every fight that is not a long-range sniper shot. The "Black Ops" aim-assist type is the strongest and most consistent preset in the current game.

Target priority and finishing

  • Shoot the cracked player, then the closest, then the most exposed. A player with no plates dies in a fraction of the time and removes a gun from the fight.
  • After a down, do not stare at the body. Their teammates know exactly where you are. Plate, move to a new position, and expect the push. Thirst only when your squad confirms it is safe, or use the downed player as bait.
  • Tracking an enemy who is sliding: aim at the end of the slide, not the body during it. Slides end predictably.

Drill · 15 minutes

Load Resurgence. For one match, refuse to shoot anyone unless you can name your fallback cover out loud first. Count how many "no fallback" fights you would have taken. That number is the leak.

Plunder or a multiplayer bot match: walk through three buildings with the reticle glued to head height on every corner. If it drops to the floor, restart the building. Boring for exactly one week, then permanent.

03 / Movement

Move to not be shot, not to look good

One rule covers 80 percent of movement: move when they cannot see you, stop and shoot when they can. Everything below is technique in service of that rule.

Rules that changed with Black Ops 7

  • No default tac sprint. Base sprint is faster than before. If you want the extra burst for rotations, run the Tac Sprinter perk; if you would rather spend the slot on Ghost or a survivability perk, that is a legitimate trade.
  • Wall jumps chain up to three times and lose distance each time. They are for reaching ledges and roofs in one motion, and for changing the angle you appear from. They are not for fighting: you cannot aim mid-jump.
  • Slide, dive and wall jump lock you out of aiming unless a perk removes the restriction. So: slide to cover, not through a doorway into a room with an enemy in it.
  • Roll after a dive in Warzone to keep momentum and recover faster.

Techniques worth owning

Slide cancel

Slide, then crouch (or jump) the instant the slide begins to cancel it and reset your sprint. Use it to change speed unpredictably while crossing a gap, and to peek a corner low and snap back. Do not spam it in the open: it is loud and slows you overall.

Jiggle and shoulder peek

Show a shoulder for a fraction of a second to bait a shot and see where they hold, then peek wide from a different edge once they are reloading or re-adjusting. Two jiggles, then commit or leave.

Mantle, climb, ladders

Mantling is fast and silent; ladders are slow and lock your gun away. Never take a ladder with an enemy within 30 metres. Look for stacked crates, awnings and wall jumps instead.

Parachute and redeploy

Cut the chute low for speed on the initial drop, re-open just before impact. Redeploy balloons and ascenders let you reposition or escape a bad circle. On Avalon, Relay Antennas grant a wingsuit launch that covers half the map; find the nearest one the moment you land.

Vehicles

  • Vehicles win rotations and lose fights. Use them to cross the map early or to beat a far circle; ditch them 100 metres before the destination and arrive on foot.
  • Never drive into the last three circles. Every gun in the lobby hears you.
  • A vehicle parked between you and the enemy is cover. A vehicle you are sitting in is a coffin with a horn.
Positioning beats movement

Flashy movement wins clips; it loses matches when it puts you in the open with no plates. If you find yourself sliding around in an open field, the mistake happened 10 seconds earlier when you left cover without a destination.

Drill · 10 minutes

Private match or empty Plunder area. Pick two pieces of cover 20 metres apart. Cross between them ten times: sprint, slide into the cover, cancel, stand, aim at head height on the far edge. Then add a wall jump onto a ledge and immediately find cover. Speed comes after cleanliness.

04 / Positioning & rotations

Be somewhere good before they are

This chapter is worth more placement than any loadout. Rotations decide which fights you are offered; positioning decides whether you get to accept them on your terms.

What a power position is

  • Hard cover you can stand fully behind, not a bush or a fence.
  • Elevation over the ground people must cross. Rooftops are strong against ground pushes and weak against snipers on higher ground, so know which one you are.
  • Two exits. One entrance means one grenade ends you.
  • Sightlines on the likely rotation path into the next circle, not on scenery.
  • Inside or on the edge of the predicted next zone, so you hold while others move.

Rotating: early, along the edge, with the gas at your back

  • Pull up the map every time the zone timer resets. Zones bias toward the centre of the current circle; a circle touching an edge tends to pull away from it. Mark a destination, then a fallback destination.
  • Start moving with at least 60 seconds of buffer when you are far, more if the route needs a vehicle. Late rotations force you across open ground into pre-aimed guns.
  • Gas at your back removes an entire angle. Holding the edge means the only players who can shoot you are in front of you. Holding the centre means everyone rotating in has a shot at you.
  • Never cross open ground without a reason and a plan. Use a vehicle, a smoke, the gas edge, or a teammate watching the far side. If none exists, take the long way.
  • Sitting in gas for five extra seconds with plates ready can beat rushing into an occupied position. Gas is predictable; a pre-aimed AR is not.

Third parties are the default, not bad luck

Every fight rings a bell for every squad within 200 metres. Assume company within 30 to 60 seconds of your first shot. That gives you three choices: finish fast (commit with the whole squad), leave (take the free reposition while two squads are busy with each other), or reset (plate, move one building over, and let the third party arrive into the fight you just left). Choosing none of them, standing where the fight started and looting, is how most intermediate squads die between circles two and four.

Late game

  • Count squads, not players. Ten players left could be three squads; four squads means chaos and you should hold. Two squads means you can take space.
  • Do not be the first to break cover in the final circle. The player who moves first eats every bullet. Let the gas do the moving; hold until a gap is guaranteed.
  • In the final two circles, a UAV before the last push and a Precision Airstrike or Cluster on the strongest position is worth more than a fourth plate satchel.
  • Snipers in the endgame: expose only to shoot and leave the window. Hold angles with an AR, not the sniper.

Resurgence and Avalon specifics

Resurgence

  • Height and a lockable building beat kills. Own a rooftop with two stairwells, hold with two, roam with one.
  • Redeploys land near teammates: fight when you have a respawn coming, disengage when you do not.
  • The game is won in the last 90 seconds when respawns stop. Have plates, a self-revive, and a plan for the final position before then.

Avalon

  • Verticality is the whole map. Relay Antennas give a wingsuit launch; use them to take high ground or to skip a bad circle entirely.
  • Zombie zones carry premium loot and attract squads; go early or not at all.
  • Consensus strong landing spots: Low Town, Colosseum, Resorts, High Town, Cliff Town. Trinity Stadium is the hot drop.
Drill · every BR match this week

Each time the zone timer resets, say out loud where you are going, why, and which cover you will use if shot on the way. Your squad will hate it for two games and then start doing it too.

05 / Information

Know more than they do

Every fight you win "because they didn't see you" was an information win. Buy information, harvest it, and share it.

Sources, in order of value

UAV and Advanced UAV

The single best purchase before any late push. Pop it when you are ready to act on it, not while looting. An Advanced UAV in the final two circles tells you every angle to pre-aim.

Recon contract

Reveals the next zone early. Do one in the first two circles and rotate before anyone else knows where to go. The best contract for placement.

Minimap and kill feed

Unsuppressed fire shows on the minimap. Downs and kills in the feed tell you where fights are and how many squads are still full. Read it every 20 seconds.

Sound

Footsteps, plating, chute pops, vehicles, redeploy balloons. Fix your audio mix (see Settings) and stop moving for one second to listen before you enter a building.

Perks and equipment

Tracker shows footprints. Alertness warns when you are being aimed at. Sensor Dart paints a building. One squadmate on information perks pays for the whole squad.

Counting

Players left divided by squad size gives you squads left. Circle number tells you how long they have to be near you. A bounty on your squad means someone knows where you are.

Suppressors are information denial

Unsuppressed shots reveal you on the minimap of everyone in range. Long-range builds in the current meta run a suppressor by default for exactly this reason: you take a fight without telling the two squads next door.

Drill · one session

Play three Resurgence matches with the goal of buying and using a UAV before every push. Track how many fights you started with a UAV up. Aim for all of them by match three.

Should an intermediate player snipe?

Only if you already hit the first shot more often than not. A missed sniper shot tells the enemy where you are and gives them a free re-plate. If your sniper hit rate is low, run two automatic weapons this season and add the sniper back once your positioning gets you uncontested angles.

Black Ops 7 perks come in three slots drawn from the Enforcer, Recon and Strategist archetypes. Overkill is built in, so both weapons are primaries and there is no Gunfighter wildcard. The exact slot each perk sits in is shown in the loadout menu; pick one answer to each question below.

How do I move and handle?

  • Tac Sprinter: brings tactical sprint back for rotations
  • Dexterity or Lightweight: handling and speed
  • Mountaineer: fall damage and drop-downs; reworked to be genuinely useful

What do I know or hide?

  • Ghost: hidden from UAVs while moving; the default late-game pick
  • Tracker: enemy footprints, ping on hit
  • Alertness: warns when someone is aiming at you (one per squad)
  • Bomb Squad: see equipment through walls, less explosive damage

How do I survive?

  • Tempered: two plates fill you instead of three; the highest-value perk for fighters
  • Reactive Armor or Plate Hunter: plate regen or an extra reserve plate
  • Field Medic: revives 25 percent faster (replaced Veteran this season)

07 / Economy & contracts

Loot faster

  • Open a building with a purpose: plates, a satchel, cash, a self-revive. Once you have them, leave. The loot that wins games is bought, not found.

08 / Modes & maps

Play each mode for what it teaches

Resurgence for gunfight reps, Battle Royale for decisions, Black Ops Royale for looting under pressure, Ranked for consistency. Use them deliberately.

Battle Royale (Verdansk · Avalon rotation)

  • Land on the edge of a POI, not the centre, unless you want the fight.
  • Gulag: the Season 5 Rush layout rotates on weekends. Stand still for the first second and listen, then pick a lane. Grab the overtime flag only when the enemy is out of position. If the "jailbreaker" event triggers, both of you return.
  • Verdansk landmarks to learn cold: Stadium, Superstore, Downtown, Hospital, Airport, Storage Town, Boneyard, Prison, Port, Quarry, Dam, Military Base, TV Station, Farmland, Lumber, Train Station, Promenade, Hills, Park, and the new Drone Labs.

Resurgence (Rebirth · Fortune's Keep · Haven's Hollow)

  • Play it for volume: 15 to 25 gunfights per match versus three in BR.
  • One focus per match (plating behind cover, pre-aim, callouts). Ignore the scoreboard for that match.
  • Rebirth: Prison and Control Center are the power positions. Fortune's Keep: Keep and the Town rooftops. Learn two rooftops with two exits each per map.

Black Ops Royale (Avalon)

  • No loadouts: weapons come with fixed archetypes and five-step rarity upgrade paths. Upgrading your current gun beats swapping.
  • No Gulag, no Buy Stations. One respawn token each, then Redeploy Towers that revive the squad and sound an alarm.
  • Perks are consumables. Stow them and pop them at the fight, not while looting. Trauma Kits push health above the cap.
  • Grapple plus wall jump plus wingsuit: it teaches vertical movement faster than any other mode.

Ranked Play: Resurgence

  • Qualify with 20 top placements (top 15 in BR or top 6 in Resurgence).
  • Divisions: Bronze, Silver (900 SR), Gold (2,100), Platinum (3,600), Diamond (5,400), Crimson (7,500), Iridescent (10,000), Top 250.
  • 150 SR cap per match. A deployment fee from Silver up means placement pays the rent and kills pay the profit. Kills on higher-ranked players pay up to 4 SR.
  • Climb Bronze to Gold on aggression, then slow down: from Platinum, dying early costs more than a third kill earns.
Weekly mix that works

Two Resurgence sessions for reps, one Battle Royale session for decisions, one session of whatever you enjoy. Ranked once your Resurgence K/D is above 1.2 and stable; before that it is an expensive way to learn what Resurgence already teaches.

09 / Squad play

Three average players beat one great one

Most squad wipes start with one player being 40 metres from the others. Comms and spacing are the cheapest skill in the game.

Callout format

Say it in this order, every time: count, direction or landmark, distance, state. "Two, on the red roof north, 60 metres, one cracked." Compass bearings from the top of your screen beat "over there". Ping while you talk.

Roles that stay stable

Caller

Decides rotations and when to take or leave a fight. One voice. Others can disagree before the decision, not during it.

Anchor

Holds the power position with the long-range gun, watches the flank, calls the third party. Rarely first to die.

Entry

First through the door with the SMG and the stun. Only goes when the anchor confirms the angle is clear and a teammate is within trade range.

Spacing and trading

  • Stay within trade range: close enough that if one of you gets shot the other can shoot back within two seconds. Usually 10 to 20 metres, one piece of cover apart.
  • Never split for loot across open ground. Loot the same block, then move together.
  • Revives: smoke or Bubble Shield first, then pick up. A revive in the open trades one down for two.
  • Share: plates and cash go to whoever is entry. A squad with one fully stocked entry beats a squad with three half-stocked players.
  • Death comms are the most valuable thing a dead player does: position of the killer, weapon, plate state, direction they moved. Say it fast and stop talking.

Playing solo in squads mode

If you queue with randoms, become the anchor. Ping everything, call your rotations even if nobody answers, and never follow a random into a building he cannot describe. Playing true Solos is a different game: fewer fights, more edge play, and a self-revive before every circle.

Gameplay toggles worth checking

  • Interact/Reload behaviour: Prioritize Interact if you keep reloading instead of opening doors; Tap to Reload otherwise.
  • Armor plate behaviour: Apply All so you can hold the button and keep looking around.
  • Slide/dive behaviour: Tap to slide, hold to dive. Diving by accident in a fight is a death.
  • Auto-move forward and sprint assist: off, they fight your own inputs in close quarters.
  • Ping wheel on a paddle or a mouse button. Callouts with a ping are twice as fast.

13 / Glossary

Words your squad will use

Anchor
The player who holds the power position and the long-range gun while others push.
Cracked
An enemy whose plates are gone. Call it every time; it changes who the squad shoots.
Head glitch
Waist-high cover that exposes only the head. Strong to hold, terrible to fight into.
High Value Loot Zone
A marked area with dense, better-than-average loot. In Season 5, Verdansk's Stadium.
Jiggle peek
A quick partial exposure to bait a shot or spot an enemy without committing.
Power position
Hard cover, elevation, two exits, sightlines on the rotation path, inside the next zone.
Rank delta
Ranked Play rule that pays more SR for kills on higher-division players.
Redeploy Tower
Black Ops Royale contract that revives the whole squad and sounds an alarm.
Relay Antenna
Avalon structure that grants a wingsuit launch for long repositions.
Reset
Leave the fight, plate, and re-engage from a new position. The most under-used call in the game.
Rotational aim assist
The strongest part of controller aim assist; it only works while the left stick is moving.
Thirst
Finishing a downed player. Safe only when the squad confirms it.
Third party
A squad that joins a fight already in progress. Assume one is coming.
Trade
Killing the player who just downed your teammate, because you were close enough to shoot back.
Trauma Kit
Consumable that pushes health above the normal maximum for a short time.
