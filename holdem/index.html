Hand log

    Every deal is played twice with the seats swapped and the identical deck, so the cards cancel and only the decisions are left. The interval is a paired 95% confidence bound — if it straddles zero, the result means nothing yet.

    What this is

    A study tool. It analyses poker situations away from the table: work out whether a call is profitable, see what a range is actually made of, and test strategy ideas against opponents that never get tired or tilt.

    What it is not

    It is not connected to any poker site and it never will be. Reading another site's screen and acting for you is against the rules everywhere that matters, and it takes money from people who believe they are playing a human. That is cheating rather than engineering, and it also tends to end in a seized balance. The maths here is the useful, legitimate part — and it is the part that actually makes you a better player.

    How much to trust each piece

    • Hand evaluator — exact. Verified against all 2,598,960 five-card hands.
    • Equity — exact when the spot is small enough to enumerate, otherwise sampled with the error bar shown. Matches published equities.
    • Push/fold — a genuine Nash equilibrium for the jam-or-fold game, residually exploitable for well under 0.005 bb.
    • The bot — a strong heuristic, not a solved strategy. It beats every baseline here, but its bluff frequency is fixed and its opponent model is crude, so a thinking human will find lines against it.

    Running it yourself

    The engine is plain ES modules under engine/ with no dependencies. npm test runs the suite; npm run build-preflop regenerates the 169×169 equity table.
