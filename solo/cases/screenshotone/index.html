Overview

A developer API that renders any URL, HTML or Markdown into a screenshot, with ad and cookie-banner blocking, full-page capture and video. Dmytro Krasun launched it solo in May 2022 after leaving a server-side engineering job. He grew it to about 1,000 customers and ~$32K MRR, then growth went flat. He publicly analyzed that plateau with Jason Cohen.

Origin

Krasun, a backend engineer, left his job and chose a market where people already paid to solve the problem: rendering web pages to images. He launched on May 29, 2022, got his first customer at $7/mo in July 2022, and kept shipping features based on customer requests.

How it makes money

Tiered monthly plans by screenshot volume. Reported plans: free (100 screenshots/mo), Basic $17/mo (2,000), Growth $79/mo (10,000), Scale $259/mo (50,000), with per-screenshot overage and two months free on annual billing.

What drove growth

  • Search intent. Coverage reports that most new customers come from high-intent Google queries like 'screenshot API'.
  • Free utilities and open documentation earned backlinks that lift the paid API's search ranking.
  • Many small integrations and SDKs, and later a CLI and an MCP server, made it easy to adopt from different stacks.
  • Steady public reporting of milestones ($10K, $20K, $32K MRR) on X and WIP kept the product visible to indie developers.

Moat & risks

What protects it

A reliable product that renders pages at scale, handles banners, lazy loading and anti-bot quirks, and has earned years of search ranking. It is a boring, hard-to-get-right product, not one with deep defensibility.

What could hurt it

The market has a ceiling. On the Jason Cohen call, the numbers showed ~9% monthly churn capping MRR near $40K, and no single large, repeatable buyer segment had appeared. Cheap competitors and self-hosted Playwright/Puppeteer also put pressure on price.

Lessons you can reuse

  1. Pick a boring category where people already pay. It removes demand risk, and an existing category makes SEO easy.
  2. Churn sets a ceiling: max MRR is roughly new MRR per month divided by the churn rate. Measure it before you plan to 3x.
  3. Free tools and good documentation work as the marketing for an API business.
  4. A usage-based API with small plans brings many small customers. Growing past a plateau usually takes a new buyer segment, not more of the same traffic.

Rebuild plan · idea to build next

A compliance evidence capture API that takes timestamped, hashed and archived captures of web pages (ads, pricing pages, cookie banners, terms) for legal, ad-compliance and brand-protection teams.

What to build

General screenshot APIs compete on price. Regulated buyers such as ad compliance, consumer-protection disputes and affiliate monitoring pay for tamper-evident chain of custody, which generic tools don't offer.

02

30-day path to a first paying customer

  1. Wrap Playwright captures with SHA-256 hashing, RFC 3161 timestamping and WARC export
  2. Add scheduled captures across geographies/proxies with a diff view
  3. Interview 10 affiliate-compliance or brand-protection managers and pre-sell a $99-299/mo plan
  4. Ship a one-page 'evidence report' PDF, since that is the artifact buyers forward to lawyers

This plan is our idea, not the founder's, and the market notes are our judgment. We haven't validated the demand: talk to 10 potential buyers before you write code.
