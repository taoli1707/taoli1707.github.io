Overview

Photopea is a free web image editor built by Ukrainian developer Ivan Kutskir that opens PSD and other formats in the browser. It earns mainly from display ads plus an ad-free premium option. Kutskir told AIN he earned about $500K in 2020 and nearly $1M over the 12 months to April 2021; later outside reports put 2024 revenue near $3M (estimate).

Origin

Kutskir began building a PSD viewer in 2012 as a side project while studying, then extended it into an editor. Photopea was released in September 2013. He added ads in 2017 once it had around 5,000 daily users, at first earning about $500 a month.

How it makes money

Display ads to free users, a paid Premium option that removes ads (listed at about $5/month), and licensing deals with other companies.

What drove growth

  • Solved a specific need: opening and editing PSD files for free without installing Photoshop
  • Posted in Photoshop-related subreddits and ran Reddit AMAs (e.g. 2018)
  • Kept the product free, so users shared it in schools and design communities
  • Ad revenue reportedly doubled roughly every six months after 2017
  • Very low running costs (hosting in the hundreds of dollars per year) let almost all revenue be profit

Moat & risks

What protects it

Years of solo work on file-format compatibility (PSD, XCF, Sketch and more) that is hard to replicate, plus strong search presence as the free Photoshop alternative.

What could hurt it

Heavy reliance on ad networks and search traffic; Adobe's own web and free tiers or AI image tools could erode usage.

Lessons you can reuse

  1. A free tool that replaces an expensive incumbent for a narrow job can grow without marketing spend
  2. Ads work when usage is large and sessions are long
  3. Long pre-revenue periods are survivable when costs are near zero
  4. Doing all the code yourself can make maintenance and fixes faster

Rebuild plan · idea to build next

A free browser tool that opens and edits a proprietary professional file format with no good free viewer, such as CAD/DWG or InDesign packages, monetized by ads and a pro tier

What to build

Browsers (WebAssembly, WebGPU) can now handle heavy files, and many people receive these files occasionally without owning the expensive software.

02

30-day path to a first paying customer

  1. Pick one format and build a read-only viewer that opens common files in the browser
  2. Post it in subreddits and forums where people ask how to open that format
  3. Add one basic edit or export feature users request most
  4. Add a single ad slot and a $5/month ad-free option

This plan is our idea, not the founder's, and the market notes are our judgment. We haven't validated the demand: talk to 10 potential buyers before you write code.
