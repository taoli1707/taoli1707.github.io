Overview

Easlo is the brand of Jason Chin, who sells Notion templates such as the Second Brain system. He reported $239K revenue in 2022 as a one-person operation and said in March 2023 he was halfway to $1M in cumulative revenue.

Origin

Chin taught himself Notion from YouTube while a student. In summer 2021 he released free templates (budget and habit trackers) on Gumroad with an optional tip and promoted them on Twitter and Product Hunt. After receiving tips of up to $100, he began charging for new templates in November 2021.

How it makes money

One-time purchases of Notion templates on Gumroad. The flagship Second Brain template has been sold at $130 and reportedly passed $100K in sales on its own; smaller single-purpose templates sell for less.

What drove growth

  • Released templates free with 'pay what you want' first, building a list of past downloaders before charging
  • Targeted a narrow starting niche (students organizing school, money and health)
  • Posted template screenshots and short threads on X, and later Instagram and TikTok
  • Bundled smaller templates into a premium all-in-one Second Brain product at a higher price

Moat & risks

What protects it

Weak on the product (templates are easy to copy) but strong on distribution: a large social following and brand recognition in the Notion template niche.

What could hurt it

Depends on Notion's popularity and on Notion's own template marketplace; AI tools that generate workspaces could commoditize templates.

Lessons you can reuse

  1. Launching free with an optional tip is a cheap way to test which product people value
  2. Start with a niche you are part of (he was a student making student templates)
  3. A bundle of existing pieces can be the highest-priced product
  4. Visual products sell well on visual social platforms; screenshots are the ad

Rebuild plan · idea to build next

Operating templates for a specific regulated small business (e.g. solo physiotherapists or tutors) in Notion or Airtable, covering intake, scheduling and compliance records

01

What to build

General productivity templates are saturated, but vertical templates that encode a profession's actual workflow are rare and buyers can expense them.

02

30-day path to a first paying customer

  1. Interview 5 practitioners in one profession about their admin workflow
  2. Build one template for their most painful workflow and give it to those 5 free
  3. Publish a $49 version on Gumroad and post walkthrough videos in that profession's groups
  4. Add a paid setup call as an upsell

This plan is our idea, not the founder's, and the market notes are our judgment. We haven't validated the demand: talk to 10 potential buyers before you write code.
