Overview

ShipFast is a paid Next.js starter codebase with authentication, Stripe payments, database, emails and SEO pages already wired up. Marc Lou launched it on September 1, 2023, packing two years of shipping knowledge into a GitHub repo. It made $40K in its first month and peaked at $133K in monthly revenue in April 2024. His Stripe-verified TrustMRR page shows $1.27M all-time revenue but only about $2K in the last 30 days as of September 2026.

Origin

Lou, a French developer who had been shipping many small startups in public, packaged the code he kept rewriting for each new project into a boilerplate. He announced it on August 31, 2023 with '$50 off for the first 50 customers', and told his wife they'd be lucky to make $100. It made $40,000 by the end of the first month.

How it makes money

One-time purchase for lifetime access to the GitHub repo and updates. As of September 2026 TrustMRR lists Starter at $199 and All-in at $249 (discounted from $299 and $349), plus a ShipFast + CodeFast bundle at $299. It also works as an entry product in Lou's portfolio (CodeFast, DataFast, TrustMRR).

What drove growth

  • Built in public on X with frequent revenue posts to a fast-growing indie-hacker audience.
  • Customer testimonials and 'shipped with ShipFast' posts turned buyers into promoters. He cited 4,200+ developers in August 2024 and 7,200+ by January 2026.
  • Named Product Hunt Maker of the Year 2023, which added credibility and backlinks.
  • YouTube videos in 2024 pushed monthly revenue to its $133K peak in April 2024, he says.
  • Tested paid ads: he reported $8,345 in Facebook ads bringing in $23,283 of ShipFast revenue (April 2024).
  • Cross-sold into his other products (CodeFast course, DataFast analytics) as ShipFast declined.

Moat & risks

What protects it

Weak on the code itself, since boilerplates are easy to copy and many clones exist. The defensibility is Lou's audience and brand recognition among indie hackers.

What could hurt it

AI coding tools now generate boilerplate on demand, which undercuts the core value, and ShipFast's sales have collapsed from $133K in April 2024 to about $2K a month in 2026. Revenue depends on the founder's personal reach.

Lessons you can reuse

  1. Package the code you already reuse. The fastest product to build is one you have effectively written several times.
  2. A one-time developer product can spike quickly on an audience but decays without new distribution.
  3. Use a successful product as a funnel into newer products (courses, SaaS) before it plateaus.
  4. Share exact ad-spend numbers: the Facebook ads result was itself a viral post.

01

What to build

Generic SaaS boilerplates cover auth and Stripe but not LLM cost metering, tenant isolation or evals, which every B2B AI app now has to rebuild.

02

Market

Buyers are developers and small agencies building B2B AI products, reached through X, GitHub and AI-engineering Discords and newsletters. Competitors include ShipFast, Supastarter and Makerkit, plus free open-source templates and AI coding tools that generate boilerplate on demand.

03

30-day path to a first paying customer

  1. Extract the metering, billing and audit pieces from an AI app you've already built into a clean repo.
  2. Write a public post comparing the cost of building them from scratch with using the kit.
  3. Pre-sell at $149 to your network and on X or relevant Discords before polishing.
  4. Record a 20-minute walkthrough video as the main sales asset.
