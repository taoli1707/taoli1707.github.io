Overview

ShipFast is a paid Next.js starter codebase with authentication, Stripe payments, database, emails and SEO pages already wired up. Marc Lou built it in about a week in 2023 from code he had reused across his own projects. He reported $384k in its first 7 months and $1M+ lifetime, with monthly sales declining since the 2024 peak.

Origin

Lou, a French developer who had been shipping many small startups in public, packaged the code he kept rewriting for each new project. He launched it around Aug 31/Sept 1 2023 with '$50 off for the first 50 customers', and told his wife they'd be lucky to make $100.

How it makes money

One-time purchase for lifetime access to the GitHub repo and updates. Tiers have ranged from about $169 to $299 depending on whether they include a private Discord community and his CodeFast course. It also works as the entry product in Lou's portfolio (CodeFast, DataFast, TrustMRR).

What drove growth

  • Built in public on X with frequent revenue posts to a fast-growing indie-hacker audience.
  • Leaderboards and 'customers who shipped with ShipFast' posts turned buyers into promoters. He reported 4,000 developers by July 2024 and 5,000+ by Nov 2024.
  • Named Product Hunt Maker of the Year 2023, which added credibility and backlinks.
  • Tested paid ads: he reported $8,345 in Facebook ads bringing in $23,283 of ShipFast revenue (April 2024).
  • Cross-sold into his other products (CodeFast course, DataFast analytics) to offset ShipFast's decline.

Moat & risks

What protects it

Weak on the code itself, since boilerplates are easy to copy and many clones exist. The defensibility is Lou's audience, the community Discord and brand recognition among indie hackers.

What could hurt it

AI coding tools now generate boilerplate on demand, which undercuts the core value, and ShipFast's monthly sales have fallen well below the 2024 peak. Revenue depends on the founder's personal reach.

Lessons you can reuse

  1. Package the code you already reuse. The fastest product to build is one you have effectively written several times.
  2. A one-time developer product can spike quickly on an audience but decays without new distribution.
  3. Use a successful product as a funnel into newer products (courses, SaaS) before it plateaus.
  4. Share exact ad-spend numbers: the Facebook ads result was itself a viral post.

What to build

30-day path to a first paying customer

  1. Extract the metering, billing and audit pieces from an AI app you've already built into a clean repo.
  2. Write a public post comparing the cost of building them from scratch with using the kit.
  3. Pre-sell at $149 to your network and on X or relevant Discords before polishing.
  4. Record a 20-minute walkthrough video as the main sales asset.

This plan is our idea, not the founder's, and the market notes are our judgment. We haven't validated the demand: talk to 10 potential buyers before you write code.
