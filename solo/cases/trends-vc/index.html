Overview

Trends.vc is a one-person research newsletter where Dru Riley publishes reports on one emerging market or trend at a time. Free readers get a summary and paid Trends Pro members get full reports and a community. He told the Indie Hackers podcast in September 2020 that it had reached $20K MRR in under a year, and it made $38,000 in October 2020.

Origin

Riley, a software developer (Rails, Scala, Spark), quit his job in February 2017 with $250,000 in savings and went 37 months without income while traveling and trying projects. In fall 2019 he worked on a B2B SaaS marketing playbook and StrongStack, which he pivoted to SaaSReport in January 2020. To 'release steam' he started writing about trends and published the first Trends report, on cloud kitchens, on February 10, 2020.

How it makes money

Trends Pro membership (full reports, databases, community, masterminds). The price went from $12/month in May 2020 to $149/year in August 2020 and $297/year in October 2020; Growth in Reverse listed the annual plan at $299/year in 2023. Sponsorships of the free newsletter were added in February 2022, and top reports are also sold as a bundle.

What drove growth

  • Used a fixed, recognizable report format (Problem, Players, Predictions, Opportunities, Haters, Links) capped at 1,000 words
  • Tested the homepage email opt-in for months from May 2020, after seeing another founder report a 40% opt-in rate
  • Two Product Hunt launches produced the biggest jumps in subscribers; the first took the list from about 7,000 to 25,000 within days, and it reached #1 on Product Hunt in August 2020
  • Was active on Indie Hackers and in the Zero To One Makers mastermind, whose members shared the reports
  • Split each report into a free half and a paid half after a paid-only pre-order got zero sales
  • Raised prices several times as the report archive grew

Moat & risks

What protects it

A large archive of researched reports and a paid community of founders; the format and consistency are hard to copy quickly.

What could hurt it

AI research tools can produce passable market summaries cheaply, pressuring the value of reports. Output depends on one researcher's time.

Lessons you can reuse

  1. A strict, repeatable format makes a newsletter recognizable and faster to produce
  2. Optimize the signup page before spending effort on traffic
  3. Sell depth to the most engaged 1-2% of readers and keep summaries free
  4. Failed products can point to what you actually enjoy producing

Rebuild plan · idea to build next

A paid research newsletter on emerging opportunities in one industry vertical, e.g. independent pharmacies or small manufacturing, with supplier and regulation data

01

What to build

General founder-trend newsletters are crowded, but owners in traditional industries lack concise research on shifts affecting them and will pay for it.

02

Market

Buyers are owners and operators in the chosen vertical (e.g. independent pharmacy owners), reached through trade associations, industry Facebook and LinkedIn groups, and conference talks. Competitors are trade publications and associations' member reports, general newsletters such as Trends.vc and Exploding Topics, and AI research tools.

03

30-day path to a first paying customer

  1. Define a fixed 6-section report format
  2. Publish 3 free reports and post them in industry groups and forums
  3. Put a signup page up and test headlines until a clear conversion rate is found
  4. Offer a paid tier with full data tables for $20-30/month after report 4
04

Tech stack

  • Ghost or beehiiv
  • Stripe
  • Airtable or Postgres
  • Perplexity or OpenAI API
  • Metabase
05

Revenue model

Free summary newsletter with a paid tier at $20-30/month or $199-$299/year for full reports and data tables, plus one-off report sales around $49.

This plan is our idea, not the founder's, and the market notes are our judgment. We haven't validated the demand: talk to 10 potential buyers before you write code.
