Overview

Better Sheets sells a membership to hundreds of screencast tutorials, templates and small tools that help people get more out of Google Sheets. Andrew Kamphey launched it after a 24-hour sprint in April 2020 and reported $200K in total revenue by its third anniversary. Most of that came from lifetime-access purchases. Note that the revenue figure is a three-year total, not an annual run rate.

Origin

Kamphey launched Better Sheets on April 3, 2020 after a 24-hour sprint to ship something. He sold lifetime access to Google Sheets tutorial videos. It made $390 from 13 sales in April 2020 and $2,291 from 134 sales in May 2020, and he kept recording tutorials based on what members asked for.

How it makes money

Mostly lifetime access. It started at $49 lifetime (or $19/mo) and was later raised to $109 lifetime. A $19/mo access pass is also offered. Lifetime deals made up about 92% of revenue in his 2023 write-up, and AppSumo campaigns were a major channel.

What drove growth

  • AppSumo lifetime deals put the product in front of a large audience of buyers already looking for tools.
  • Answering members' requests with new tutorials kept growing the library, which justified raising the lifetime price.
  • YouTube, a newsletter and posts on Indie Hackers and LinkedIn documented each milestone ($100K, then $200K).
  • He went full time in 2022 after about $100K in two years.

Moat & risks

What protects it

A large back catalog of focused Sheets tutorials and templates plus a recognizable personal brand ('the Sheets guy'). The content can be copied piece by piece, but the full library and audience are harder to copy.

What could hurt it

Lifetime pricing makes revenue spiky and front-loaded, with no recurring base. AI assistants inside Google Sheets (Gemini) can answer many 'how do I' questions for free.

Lessons you can reuse

  1. Teaching a ubiquitous tool (Sheets) to a narrow audience can be a business on its own.
  2. Lifetime deals raise cash fast but are not recurring revenue. Keep track of which one you are building.
  3. Let members' questions set the content roadmap. Each answered question becomes a new tutorial.
  4. Ship in 24 hours, then raise prices as the library grows.

Idea to build next

A library of Google Sheets and Apps Script automations and tutorials for small bookkeeping and accounting practices, such as bank CSV cleanup, client reconciliation trackers and AI-assisted categorization scripts.

Why now: General Sheets tutorials now compete with built-in AI help. Workflow packs for one profession, bundled with working scripts, still save hours and are easy to pay for.

30-day path to a first paying customer

  1. Interview 10 solo bookkeepers about their most repeated spreadsheet chore
  2. Build 5 working template+script bundles and a short screencast for each
  3. Sell a $99/yr membership (plus a lifetime launch price) through bookkeeper Facebook groups and Reddit
  4. Publish one free tutorial per week for SEO and YouTube

This is our idea, not the founder's. We haven't validated the demand: talk to 10 potential buyers before you write code.
