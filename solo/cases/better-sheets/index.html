Overview

Better Sheets sells a membership to hundreds of screencast tutorials, templates and small tools that help people get more out of Google Sheets. Andrew Kamphey launched it after a 24-hour sprint in April 2020, passed $100K in top-line revenue after two years and $200K by its third anniversary, mostly from lifetime-access sales on AppSumo. By mid-2024 Indie Hackers profiled it as a ~$5K/month lifestyle business.

Origin

Kamphey spent about five years as the 'Google Sheets guy' at an entertainment startup, automating work with Apps Script, then went out on his own in 2018. After other products stalled, he launched Better Sheets on April 3, 2020 as a 24-hour project: 8 screencast videos, 4 free and 4 behind a paywall. It made $390 from 13 sales in April 2020 and $2,291 from 134 sales in May 2020, and he kept recording tutorials based on what members asked for.

How it makes money

Mostly lifetime memberships. Lifetime access was $19 for the first year or so, then $49, $69 by May 2022, $225 in late 2023 and $250 from February 2024; a monthly plan was added around 2022-23. In his two-year breakdown, lifetime memberships were about 80% of revenue and AppSumo about 85% of sales. By 2024 revenue also came from Gumroad templates, subscriptions and consulting via Stripe, Udemy courses and YouTube ads.

What drove growth

  • AppSumo's marketplace put the lifetime deal in front of a large audience of buyers already looking for tools, and did much of the marketing in exchange for its cut.
  • Answering members' requests with new tutorials kept growing the library, which justified raising the lifetime price step by step.
  • A free template pack for AppSumo users (34,000 downloads by May 2022), a Product Hunt launch that reached #1 Product of the Day in 2022, and milestone posts on Indie Hackers.
  • Later diversified into Google Sheets add-ons, small one-page tools, Udemy courses and YouTube to smooth out lumpy lifetime-deal revenue.

Moat & risks

What protects it

A large back catalog of focused Sheets tutorials and templates plus a recognizable personal brand ('the Sheets guy'). The content can be copied piece by piece, but the full library and audience are harder to copy.

What could hurt it

Lifetime pricing makes revenue spiky and front-loaded, with no recurring base. AI assistants inside Google Sheets (Gemini) can answer many 'how do I' questions for free.

Lessons you can reuse

  1. Teaching a ubiquitous tool (Sheets) to a narrow audience can be a business on its own.
  2. Lifetime deals raise cash fast but are not recurring revenue. Keep track of which one you are building.
  3. Let members' questions set the content roadmap. Each answered question becomes a new tutorial.
  4. Ship in 24 hours, then raise prices as the library grows.

Rebuild plan · idea to build next

A library of Google Sheets and Apps Script automations and tutorials for small bookkeeping and accounting practices, such as bank CSV cleanup, client reconciliation trackers and AI-assisted categorization scripts.

01

What to build

General Sheets tutorials now compete with built-in AI help. Workflow packs for one profession, bundled with working scripts, still save hours and are easy to pay for.

02

Market

Buyers are solo bookkeepers and small accounting practices, reached through bookkeeper Facebook groups, r/Bookkeeping, YouTube tutorials and SEO. Competitors include Better Sheets itself, template sellers on Etsy and Gumroad, and bookkeeping tools like QuickBooks, Xero and Dext.

03

30-day path to a first paying customer

  1. Interview 10 solo bookkeepers about their most repeated spreadsheet chore
  2. Build 5 working template+script bundles and a short screencast for each
  3. Sell a $99/yr membership (plus a lifetime launch price) through bookkeeper Facebook groups and Reddit
  4. Publish one free tutorial per week for SEO and YouTube
04

Tech stack

  • Google Apps Script
  • Google Workspace Marketplace add-on
  • Next.js
  • Stripe
  • OpenAI API
  • Loom or Screen Studio
05

Revenue model

A $99/yr membership for the full bundle library, with a limited $199 lifetime launch deal and $29-$49 single bundles on Gumroad.

This plan is our idea, not the founder's, and the market notes are our judgment. We haven't validated the demand: talk to 10 potential buyers before you write code.
