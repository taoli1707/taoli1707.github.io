Best playbooks for data engineers

Businesses and ideas built on collecting, cleaning or indexing data.

  1. Better SheetsA library of Google Sheets and Apps Script automations and tutorials for small bookkeeping and accounting practices, such as bank CSV cleanup, client reconciliation trackers and AI-assisted categorization scripts.
  2. Remote OKA job board for data engineering and analytics engineering roles, aggregated from public ATS feeds (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby), with normalized stack tags (dbt, Airflow, Spark, Snowflake) and salary filters.
  3. Nomad List (now Nomads.com)A 'where to live' data site plus paid community for remote-working families: school options, pediatric care, kid-friendly neighborhoods and visa rules for dependents, city by city.
  4. StoremapperA 'find our product near you' locator for craft breweries, wineries and specialty food brands that ingests messy distributor depletion reports (CSV/Excel) and keeps retailer locations up to date automatically.
  5. BetaListA curated directory of MCP servers and AI-agent tools with verified install and compatibility testing, a free listing queue and a paid expedited review with a badge.
  6. BuiltWithAn 'AI stack' detector that shows which LLM providers, vector databases and agent frameworks companies use, drawn from JS bundles, DNS/CNAMEs, status-page vendors and job posts, and sold as lead lists to AI tooling vendors.
  7. BannerbearA template-to-PDF and image API for real-estate agents' listing flyers and social posts, pulling data straight from MLS/IDX feeds.
  8. CSS for JavaScript DevelopersAn interactive course teaching SQL and data modeling specifically to product managers and analysts in non-tech companies
  9. GummySearchA customer-research tool built on licensed or first-party sources: public app-store reviews, G2/Capterra-style reviews the customer exports, support tickets and sales call transcripts, clustered into pain points and feature requests.
  10. ScreenshotOneA compliance evidence capture API that takes timestamped, hashed and archived captures of web pages (ads, pricing pages, cookie banners, terms) for legal, ad-compliance and brand-protection teams.
