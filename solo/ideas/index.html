Ideas to build

23 ideas borrowed from businesses that already work.

Each idea reuses a proven playbook in a niche the original doesn't serve. Easiest first. Every one links back to the case it came from, so you can see the evidence before you commit a weekend.

  1. ●●●●● Weekend project5-10 h/wk

    A library of Google Sheets and Apps Script automations and tutorials for small bookkeeping and accounting practices, such as bank CSV cleanup, client reconciliation trackers and AI-assisted categorization scripts.

    General Sheets tutorials now compete with built-in AI help. Workflow packs for one profession, bundled with working scripts, still save hours and are easy to pay for.

    Best fit: Someone with data-processing and spreadsheet skills who can record clear screencasts

    Playbook from Better Sheets · $200K lifetime in first 3 yrs (~$67K/yr avg)

  2. ●●●●● Easy5-10 h/wk

    A curated directory of MCP servers and AI-agent tools with verified install and compatibility testing, a free listing queue and a paid expedited review with a badge.

    The number of agent tools and MCP servers is growing fast, and discovery is fragmented across GitHub and X. Builders will pay for fast review and a trustworthy 'tested' badge.

    Best fit: An engineer who can automate scraping and testing pipelines and enjoys curation

    Playbook from BetaList · ~$1M lifetime over 10 yrs (~$100K/yr avg)

  3. ●●●●● Easy5-10 h/wk

    A generator for printable, standards-aligned classroom activities (word searches, bingo, flashcards) that takes a teacher's own vocabulary list and aligns to phonics or ESL curricula, sold to homeschoolers and ESL tutors.

    Generic worksheet sites don't map to specific curricula or learner levels. LLMs now make it cheap to produce structured, level-appropriate content from a list, but it needs a strict template layer to print well.

    Best fit: An engineer who can do programmatic SEO and PDF templating and knows (or can interview) teachers

    Playbook from Bingo Card Creator · >$100K sales in 2011 (~$70K profit)

  4. ●●●●● Easy8-12 h/wk

    A one-page site builder just for local tradespeople (plumbers, electricians, cleaners) with built-in quote-request forms, service-area maps and Google review embeds, at $29/yr.

    General builders force tradespeople through design choices they don't care about. Most need one page that ranks locally and collects quote requests.

    Best fit: Front-end developers who can do local outreach

    Playbook from Carrd · $1M ARR

  5. ●●●●● Easy5-10 h/wk

    Operating templates for a specific regulated small business (e.g. solo physiotherapists or tutors) in Notion or Airtable, covering intake, scheduling and compliance records

    General productivity templates are saturated, but vertical templates that encode a profession's actual workflow are rare and buyers can expense them.

    Best fit: Someone who works in or alongside that profession and knows Notion or Airtable well

    Playbook from Easlo · $239K in 2022

  6. ●●●●● Easy10-15 h/wk

    AI-generated, brand-consistent staff photos for small healthcare and real-estate practices, delivered as ready-to-upload website 'Meet the team' pages and Google Business Profile images.

    Headshot tools stop at the image file. Small practices still pay a photographer and a web designer to get consistent team pages.

    Best fit: Developers or designers comfortable with cold outreach to local businesses

    Playbook from HeadshotPro · ~$300k/month (widely reported)

  7. ●●●●● Easy8-12 h/wk

    A 'where to live' data site plus paid community for remote-working families: school options, pediatric care, kid-friendly neighborhoods and visa rules for dependents, city by city.

    Nomad-focused sites are built around single travellers. Families relocating for remote work have different, higher-stakes data needs and are willing to pay more.

    Best fit: Parents who have relocated abroad and can seed the community from lived experience

    Playbook from Nomad List (now Nomads.com) · ~$61k/month (Sept 2024)

  8. ●●●●● Easy8-12 h/wk

    A job board for data engineering and analytics engineering roles, aggregated from public ATS feeds (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby), with normalized stack tags (dbt, Airflow, Spark, Snowflake) and salary filters.

    Generic boards tag jobs poorly by data stack, and many ATS systems expose public JSON job feeds. A data engineer can build a much better-structured index than a general board.

    Best fit: A data engineer who knows the stack vocabulary and can build a reliable nightly ETL

    Playbook from Remote OK · $101K in one month (Apr 2021 peak)

  9. ●●●●● Easy8-12 h/wk

    A 'find our product near you' locator for craft breweries, wineries and specialty food brands that ingests messy distributor depletion reports (CSV/Excel) and keeps retailer locations up to date automatically.

    Generic store locators assume the brand keeps a clean store list. Beverage and food brands selling through distributors get irregular spreadsheets, so their 'where to buy' pages go stale.

    Best fit: A data engineer who enjoys messy spreadsheet ETL and geocoding

    Playbook from Storemapper · ~$25K/mo (2017 podcast)

  10. ●●●●● Easy8-12 h/wk

    A course and newsletter teaching licensed tradespeople (electricians, HVAC techs) to leave employment and run a one-van business, built from one tradesperson's own numbers

    Solopreneur education is aimed almost entirely at knowledge workers; trades have high demand and few people teaching the business side in plain language.

    Best fit: A working tradesperson who already runs their own small shop and is comfortable posting publicly

    Playbook from The Saturday Solopreneur + courses · $4.15M+ in 2024 (~86% margin)

  11. ●●●●● Moderate10-15 h/wk

    A template-to-PDF and image API for real-estate agents' listing flyers and social posts, pulling data straight from MLS/IDX feeds.

    General image APIs need a developer to wire them up. Agents and brokerages want flyers generated automatically whenever a listing changes.

    Best fit: Backend developers with some real-estate industry access

    Playbook from Bannerbear · $1M ARR

  12. ●●●●● Moderate10-15 h/wk

    An 'AI stack' detector that shows which LLM providers, vector databases and agent frameworks companies use, drawn from JS bundles, DNS/CNAMEs, status-page vendors and job posts, and sold as lead lists to AI tooling vendors.

    BuiltWith-style detectors are built around front-end technology fingerprints. Most AI adoption happens on the backend and is only visible through indirect signals such as job posts, subprocessor lists and SDK traces.

    Best fit: A data engineer comfortable with crawling, entity resolution and scheduled pipelines

    Playbook from BuiltWith · ~$14M/yr (widely reported, not audited)

  13. ●●●●● Moderate10-15 h/wk

    An interactive course teaching SQL and data modeling specifically to product managers and analysts in non-tech companies

    Many business staff now query data with AI help but lack fundamentals to check results; most SQL courses target developers or data scientists.

    Best fit: A data analyst or engineer who writes clearly and can build simple web interactives

    Playbook from CSS for JavaScript Developers · ~$550K in a one-week pre-sale (Mar 2021)

  14. ●●●●● Moderate10-15 h/wk

    A customer-research tool built on licensed or first-party sources: public app-store reviews, G2/Capterra-style reviews the customer exports, support tickets and sales call transcripts, clustered into pain points and feature requests.

    GummySearch's shutdown left users looking for alternatives, and the lesson is to avoid data you can't license. Customers' own exported data, plus sources with stable public access, avoid the same fate.

    Best fit: A data engineer comfortable with ETL from many small sources, embeddings and clustering

    Playbook from GummySearch · ~$35K MRR at shutdown

  15. ●●●●● Moderate10-15 h/wk

    A paid newsletter for a professional role that has no 'Lenny' yet, such as hospital operations managers or clinical research coordinators

    Tech roles are saturated with newsletters, but many well-paid operational roles have little practitioner-written, tactical content, and employers already pay for training.

    Best fit: A senior practitioner in the role with a network of peers who will share the first posts

    Playbook from Lenny's Newsletter · $1M+/yr newsletter + podcast combined

  16. ●●●●● Moderate10-15 h/wk

    Consistent AI product and lifestyle photos of one physical product (not a person) for small Etsy and Shopify sellers: train on 10 phone photos, then generate catalogue and lifestyle shots on demand.

    Per-subject fine-tuning is now cheap, and small sellers still pay photographers per shoot. Most AI product-photo tools do background swaps, not faithful multi-angle renders of one specific item.

    Best fit: Developers comfortable with image-model APIs who can also sell to e-commerce sellers

    Playbook from Photo AI · ~$150k/month

  17. ●●●●● Moderate10-15 h/wk

    A paid, private archive for a specific profession's web sources, such as lawyers or journalists capturing web pages as timestamped evidence

    Web pages change or disappear often, and professionals who must prove what a page said have few simple, low-cost tools outside expensive enterprise suites.

    Best fit: A web developer with contacts in journalism or legal work

    Playbook from Pinboard · ~$234K revenue in 2016, ~$17K/yr costs

  18. ●●●●● Moderate10-15 h/wk

    A compliance evidence capture API that takes timestamped, hashed and archived captures of web pages (ads, pricing pages, cookie banners, terms) for legal, ad-compliance and brand-protection teams.

    General screenshot APIs compete on price. Regulated buyers such as ad compliance, consumer-protection disputes and affiliate monitoring pay for tamper-evident chain of custody, which generic tools don't offer.

    Best fit: A backend or data engineer who likes infrastructure and can sell to non-technical compliance teams

    Playbook from ScreenshotOne · ~$32K MRR (plateaued)

  19. ●●●●● Moderate10-15 h/wk

    A production-ready starter kit for AI agent apps in regulated or B2B settings: multi-tenant auth, per-customer LLM usage metering and billing, audit logs and eval harness pre-wired.

    Generic SaaS boilerplates cover auth and Stripe but not LLM cost metering, tenant isolation or evals, which every B2B AI app now has to rebuild.

    Best fit: Engineers who have shipped a B2B AI product and have some audience on X or GitHub

    Playbook from ShipFast · $17.2k/month (Jan 2026)

  20. ●●●●● Moderate8-12 h/wk

    A lifetime-fee community and class series for early-career public servants or NGO staff building side incomes within their employer's ethics rules

    Most indie-business communities assume tech workers; government and nonprofit staff face specific conflict-of-interest rules and have no dedicated space.

    Best fit: Someone who has worked in that sector and already runs a small permitted side project

    Playbook from Small Bets · $824K community revenue over 24 months (to Oct 2023)

  21. ●●●●● Moderate10-15 h/wk

    A paid research newsletter on emerging opportunities in one industry vertical, e.g. independent pharmacies or small manufacturing, with supplier and regulation data

    General founder-trend newsletters are crowded, but owners in traditional industries lack concise research on shifts affecting them and will pay for it.

    Best fit: An analyst or consultant with background in that industry

    Playbook from Trends.vc · $20K MRR within a year of launch

  22. ●●●●● Moderate12-18 h/wk

    A bring-your-own-key AI workspace for regulated small firms (law offices, accountants, clinics) that ships with audit logs, PII redaction before prompts leave the machine, and ready-made prompt packs for each profession.

    General chat UIs target individuals and big enterprises. Small regulated firms want to use AI but need redaction and logging they can show to insurers or regulators.

    Best fit: Full-stack developers with a contact inside a regulated profession

    Playbook from TypingMind · $148k in one month (all-time high)

  23. ●●●●● Very hard15-20 h/wk

    A free browser tool that opens and edits a proprietary professional file format with no good free viewer, such as CAD/DWG or InDesign packages, monetized by ads and a pro tier

    Browsers (WebAssembly, WebGPU) can now handle heavy files, and many people receive these files occasionally without owning the expensive software.

    Best fit: A strong low-level developer comfortable reverse-engineering file formats

    Playbook from Photopea · ~$1M over 12 months (to Apr 2021)
